## Nail designs from your stylist

In Nailfolio you pick looks from your stylist's gallery, build them into an inspiration session with a note and send it before your visit. Once the two of you are connected, you message her in Messages. Start by scanning a QR code at the salon.

- Always free for clients, no card
- QR at the station
- Photos of your stylist’s work

## How to prepare inspiration before a visit

Choose photos from your stylist’s gallery, add notes and share an Inspiration Session. Your stylist can review it before you meet.

### Connect with a stylist using a QR code

Scan the QR code at the station, your stylist confirms, and her gallery opens on your phone.

At the salon

### Choose photos from your stylist’s gallery

Filters work together: pick color, style and length at the same time. You only see the designs that match your idea.

Before deciding

### Share an Inspiration Session with notes

Your stylist sees your chosen photos with notes and can prepare in advance.

Before your next visit

### Browse your stylist’s latest work

Visit your stylist’s gallery to view newly added photos.

After the visit

## Your stylist’s gallery, liked designs and Inspiration Sessions

After connecting with your stylist, browse their gallery, choose designs and share Inspiration Sessions. You can also use Messages. Client accounts are free.

### A nail gallery with color, style and length filters

Browse your stylist’s work and combine color, style and nail length filters.

Results include designs that match all selected filters.

### Your stylist's full gallery on your phone

You'll see a QR code at your stylist's station. Scan it with your phone and sign in without a password, and once your stylist confirms, her full gallery is yours.

### Your collection of favorite designs

Like the designs from your stylist's gallery and build a collection before your visit. You can return to it from any phone. Only you can see this collection until you share it with your stylist.

Your collection stays with you even when your stylist adds new work. Pick up where you left off on any phone.

### Your stylists in one place

Like the stylist profiles you want to revisit. Find them in your collection and check their public profiles for new work.

### Inspiration Session with photos and notes

Select photos and add notes in an Inspiration Session. Once shared, your stylist can review your choices before the visit.

Your stylist opens one session and sees everything at once: photos, notes and order.

### Messages to your stylist

After your connection is confirmed, you can contact your stylist in Messages.

Clients always use Nailfolio for free.

### Scan the QR code at the nail salon

Scan the code, sign in and send a connection request. Once the stylist approves it, you can access their gallery.

### Filter nail designs by color, style and length

Filters for color, style and length narrow your choice. You can like the designs you pick and add a short note.

### Share the inspiration session with your stylist

Your stylist can view selected photos and notes. A shared session is available for 7 days.

Inspiration sessions stay live for 7 days after sharing.

## Client account privacy

Nailfolio stores information needed to operate your account and connections with stylists. It does not sell data, display ads or track you across apps.

- Nailfolio does not sell data to third parties
- No ads, no cross-app tracking
- Deactivate your account with one tap, and restore it within 90 days

## Data and payments in Nailfolio

Client accounts are free and require no card. Nailfolio does not display ads. The database is hosted in the European Union.

- No hidden fees
- No credit card, no payments
- Encrypted connection
- Database hosted in the EU
- Private galleries
- No ads, no data selling

## How to invite your stylist to Nailfolio

Send your stylist a Nailfolio link. They can create a free account, no card required, and you come back to their gallery before your next visit.
