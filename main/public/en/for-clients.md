# Nail Designs From Your Stylist's Portfolio, Set Before Your Visit

> Scan your stylist's QR code, choose nail designs from her portfolio, set them in an inspiration session and message her before your visit. Nailfolio is free for clients.

## Nail designs from your stylist

Connect with your nail professional through their profile or QR code. Pick inspirations from their gallery and discuss the details before your appointment to save time at the salon.

- Your account is free, no card required

## How to prepare inspiration before a visit

Choose photos from your stylist’s gallery and share an Inspiration Session. You can add a note. Your stylist can review the session before your appointment.

### Connect with your stylist using a QR code

Scan the stylist’s QR code and send a connection request. You can view the full gallery once they accept.

### Choose photos from your stylist’s gallery

Filters work together: pick color, style and length at the same time. You only see the designs that match your idea.

### Sharing an Inspiration Session

Your stylist sees the selected photos and any note you add, so she can prepare in advance.

### Browse your stylist’s new work

Check your stylist’s gallery for new photos.

## Your stylist’s gallery, liked designs and Inspiration Sessions

Once connected to your stylist, you can browse her full gallery, choose designs and share Inspiration Sessions. You can also message her from her profile before connecting. Your account is free.

### A nail gallery with color, style and length filters

Browse your stylist’s work and combine color, style and nail length filters.

Results include designs that match all selected filters.

### Your stylist’s full gallery on your phone

Scan your stylist’s QR code and sign in without a password. Once she approves the connection, you can see her full gallery; if she contributes to an active Studio salon, you can also browse the whole contributing team’s work.

### Your collection of favorite designs

After signing in, like designs from your stylist’s gallery. You can add selected photos to an Inspiration Session and share it with your stylist.

Your collection stays with you even when your stylist adds new work. Pick up where you left off on any phone.

### Your stylists in one place

Like the stylist profiles you want to revisit. Find them in your collection and check their public profiles for new work.

### An Inspiration Session with photos

In an Inspiration Session, choose photos and optionally add a note. Once you share it, your stylist can review your selection before the appointment.

Your stylist opens the session and sees the selected photos and any note you add.

### Messages to your stylist

You can message your stylist from her public profile, even before connecting. You will find the conversation in Messages.

Use Nailfolio free of charge.

### Scan the QR code at the nail salon

Scan the code, sign in and send a connection request. Once your stylist approves it, you can access her gallery.

### Filter nail designs by color, style and length

Filters for color, style and length narrow your choice. You can like the designs you pick and add a short note.

### Share the inspiration session with your stylist

Your stylist sees the selected photos and one note. A shared session stays active for 30 days.

Inspiration sessions stay live for 30 days after sharing.

## Your account and privacy

Nailfolio stores the information needed to run your account and connections with your stylists. It does not sell data, show ads or track you across apps.

- Nailfolio does not sell data to third parties
- No ads, no cross-app tracking
- After closing your account, you have 90 days to restore it.

## How to invite your stylist to Nailfolio

Send your stylist a link to Nailfolio. They can try the Free Demo without a card, and you can return to their gallery before your next appointment.
