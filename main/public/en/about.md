## About Nailfolio: an app for nail stylists and clients

Nailfolio is developed in Kraków. Use the app to present your work, receive Inspiration Sessions from clients and record visit history.

A nail portfolio and client history for independent stylists and salons.

In Nailfolio, you maintain a public profile, work gallery and client cards. Clients choose photos for Inspiration Sessions and share them before a visit.

## Why Nailfolio was created

- Photos chosen before a visit help you discuss color, length and nail art. When a client chooses from their stylist’s work, the conversation can refer to designs the stylist has already created.
- Social media posts help you present your work and connect with an audience. Preparing a nail design also involves photo filters, notes and the individual client’s visit history.
- Nailfolio combines these features in an app for nail stylists. Clients browse a gallery with color, style and length filters and share Inspiration Sessions after connecting with a stylist. The stylist can review the selected photos and record notes and visit history in the client card.

Work gallery, inspiration and visit history.

## Gallery, mobile access and data

Features and terms of using Nailfolio.

### Work gallery for nail stylists

The gallery has nail color, style and length filters. A QR code connects a client with a stylist. Once the connection is confirmed, the client can choose photos for an Inspiration Session.

### Photos and QR code on your phone

Add a photo from your phone, select the design’s colors and features, then publish it in your gallery. Show the QR code on your screen or print it.

### Photo ownership and data storage

The app database is stored in the European Union. Nailfolio does not sell data or use marketing cookies. You retain ownership of your photos regardless of your plan.

### Who is behind Nailfolio?

Nailfolio is an independently developed product from Kraków. Feedback from nail stylists informs its development.

### How is Nailfolio different from Booksy?

Booksy is a platform for booking appointments. Nailfolio is a nail portfolio with client history: color, style and length filters, a QR code at the salon, and Inspiration Sessions from clients. They solve different problems, so the two work well side by side.

### How is Nailfolio development funded?

Nailfolio is developed without external investors. Paid plans fund further app development.

### Where is the data stored?

The app database is stored in the EU. Cloudinary handles photos and Stripe handles payments. Some providers also process data outside the EU; see the privacy policy for details. Nailfolio does not sell data or use marketing cookies.

### Which languages is Nailfolio available in?

Nailfolio is available in Polish, English and German. Planned further markets include Czechia.
