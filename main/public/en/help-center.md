## Nailfolio Help Center

Find answers about your gallery, QR code, inspiration sessions, messages, client history and your account.

## How to connect with a stylist and share inspiration

### Scan QR code

Scan your stylist’s QR code and sign in to send a connection request.

### Browse the gallery

Once your stylist confirms the connection, you can browse all her work, not just her public photos.

### Prepare your session

Like designs, add notes and share your inspiration session with your stylist before the visit.

### Discuss the design before your visit

After you share the session, your stylist can see the selected photos and notes. Discuss the details in Messages.

## General

### What is Nailfolio?

Nailfolio is an app for nail stylists and their clients. It includes a gallery with filters, QR connections, Inspiration Sessions, Messages and a client card with visit history.

### Is Nailfolio free?

The Free plan for stylists has no charge. Paid plans offer higher limits and additional features. Client accounts are free.

### How do I get started with Nailfolio?

Open nailfolio.app in your phone’s browser on Android or iPhone. Sign in with Google or a link sent by email.

### What devices does Nailfolio work on?

All app features are available in your phone’s browser on Android and iPhone. Nailfolio has no separate desktop app. The Android app is in preparation.

### Is my data safe?

Connections to the app are encrypted and the database is stored in the EU. Stripe handles payments. See the privacy policy for information about service providers and data processing.

### How can I contact Nailfolio?

Use the contact form, email kontakt@nailfolio.app or send a message on Instagram (@nailfolio).

### How do I sign in if there's no password?

Sign in with Google or a link sent by email. To receive a link, enter your email address, then open the link in the message. No password is required.

### The magic sign-in link isn't arriving, what should I do?

Check your spam and promotions folders. The login link is valid for one hour; request a new one after that time. You can also sign in with Google. If the problem persists, use the contact form and provide the email address used to sign in.

### Can I sign in on several devices at once?

Yes. The same account works on all your devices at the same time. Sign in the same way (magic link to the same email or Google). You can check and sign out active sessions in Account → Signed-in devices.

### How do I switch between the nail artist and client roles?

You can have a stylist and a client account at the same time. Go to Account → Settings → Switch role. You keep all your data, each role has its own galleries and settings.

### The app isn't working or loads slowly, what should I do?

Close and reopen Nailfolio, then check your internet connection. If the problem persists, submit a report with your phone model, operating system and a description of the error.

### How do I report an inappropriate photo?

On the photo, open the menu (three dots) and choose Report, then select a reason. An admin reviews the report within 48 hours on business days and makes a decision. You'll receive a notification about the outcome.

## For nail artists

### How do I add nail photos to my Nailfolio gallery?

Open the app, go to the Add tab, take a photo or choose from your gallery, tag colors and features, then publish.

### What are pinned photos?

Pinned photos are your best work, displayed at the top of your public profile. The number of pins depends on your plan.

### How do I generate a QR code for clients?

You have one permanent code: you will find it on the Connection screen, you can show it to a client on your phone or print it and hang it at your station. The client scans it with her camera, lands on your profile and sends a connection request, which you accept with one tap.

### How can a client send me inspiration before the visit?

The client creates an inspiration session: she picks photos from your gallery, adds notes and shares the whole collection with you in one click. You see it in the Session Clipboard in the app.

### How do I change my pricing plan?

Go to Account → Subscription. You can change your plan at any time: a higher plan takes effect immediately, a lower one at the end of the billing period.

### Can I add nail artist accounts?

Studio includes a salon team roster and invitations to existing stylist accounts. An invited stylist accepts or declines the invitation. The plan covers up to 10 accounts including the owner, with no per-seat fees. Team membership does not currently provide access to the salon account or gallery.

### What happens to my photos when I downgrade?

Your photos stay in your account and nothing is hidden: connected clients still see your whole gallery, and on the Free plan your public profile shows up to 3 pinned works. Free only stops you from adding photos beyond 5. Nothing is deleted without your decision.

### How does subscription auto-renewal work?

After the 30-day trial the subscription renews automatically. A reminder arrives 7 days before the charge, so you have time to cancel or change plans. You cancel from your account, no extra email needed.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account → Subscription → Manage subscription. The Stripe billing portal opens, where you can cancel your subscription. After cancelling you return to the Free plan and keep your account and data. Connected clients still see all your photos, and Free only stops you from adding new ones beyond the limit.

### My payment didn't go through, what should I do?

Check that your card is active and has sufficient funds. Go to Account → Subscription → Manage subscription and update your card details. Stripe will automatically retry the payment. If the problem persists, use the contact form.

### I'd like a refund, how does that work?

During the 30-day Pro trial, you can cancel without a charge. If a payment has already been taken, submit a request within 14 days. After the request is reviewed, you will receive a reply about the refund. Any approved refund is credited to your card within 5 to 10 business days.

### A staff member has left, how do I revoke their access?

Go to Account → Team, select the staff member and click Remove. The person is removed from the salon team roster. Their private Nailfolio account remains available.

### How do I reply to a client in the app?

Open the Messages tab, there you'll see conversations with clients you're connected with.

### Do I have to add a phone number to my profile?

No, the field is optional. If you fill it in, clients will see it on your public profile.

## For clients

### How do I find a nail artist?

Scan the QR code at your stylist's salon and sign in to the app with a Google account or a link sent by email. That sends a connection request. Your stylist controls access: once she confirms the request with a single tap, you'll see her whole gallery. Then you can filter the work by colors and features to quickly find what you are looking for.

### Can I like nail artists?

Yes! Click Like nail artist on her profile. You'll find her in your collection afterwards.

### How do I like a design?

Click the heart icon on a photo in the gallery. Your liked designs are in the Your collection tab.

### What is connecting with a nail artist?

Connecting with your nail artist is a quick way to see her full gallery, put together an inspiration session before your visit and message her in Messages. Scan her QR code at the salon and, once she confirms the connection, you have access to all of it.

### Do I have to pay for an account?

No! A client account is completely free. You can browse galleries and like any design or nail artist, with no charges at all.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Settings → Delete account. The account is deactivated and your data is stored safely. You have 90 days to change your mind: just log in again and restore your account with one click. Permanent data erasure is available any time on request (GDPR Art. 17) by writing to privacy@nailfolio.app.

### Does the artist’s code expire or work only once?

No. Your nail artist has one permanent code: the same one hangs at the station and shows on her screen. Scan it with your regular camera, you will land on her profile and send a connection request, and she accepts it with one tap.

### I see a message that I'm already connected with this nail artist. What does that mean?

It means you already have an active connection with this stylist and don't need to scan their code again. Open Dashboard → Connected stylists to see their full gallery. You can have up to 3 connected stylists at a time.

### My photo has disappeared from the gallery, why?

A photo may be hidden if it violates the content rules, for example by showing personal data, faces or content unrelated to nails. The reason for the decision is sent by email. To appeal, reply to that message. You will receive a reply to the appeal within 48 hours.

### Can I message my stylist?

Yes, in the Messages tab, once you're connected.

## Technical

### What photo formats are supported?

Nailfolio supports JPG, PNG and WEBP formats. Maximum file size is 10 MB. Photos are automatically optimised.

### How does color tagging work?

When adding a photo you choose colors from the palette available in the app. You can select multiple colors for one photo.

### Is data encrypted?

Connections to the app are encrypted. The database is stored in the European Union.

### What are style features?

Features are tags describing techniques, effects and styles, e.g. French, Ombre, Chrome, Floral. The app has a set of predefined features in four categories.

### How do gallery filters work?

In a stylist's gallery you filter photos by color, style and nail length. The filters combine, and results update instantly.

### Does Nailfolio have an API?

Nailfolio does not offer a public API. Submit integration questions using the contact form.

### Does Nailfolio work offline?

The gallery and Inspiration Sessions require an internet connection. Nailfolio has no offline mode.
