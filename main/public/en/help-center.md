## Nailfolio Help Center

Answers about galleries, QR codes, Inspiration Sessions, messages, client history and accounts.

## How a client shares inspiration with her stylist

### The client scans a QR code

The client scans her stylist’s QR code, signs in and sends a connection request.

### The stylist accepts the connection

After the request is accepted, the client can browse the stylist’s full gallery, including private work.

### The client chooses photos

The client chooses photos, can like designs, adds one note and shares an Inspiration Session with her stylist before the visit.

### The stylist receives the session

After the session is shared, the stylist sees the chosen photos and note. The client can discuss the details in Messages.

## General

### What is Nailfolio?

Nailfolio is an app for nail stylists and their clients. It includes a gallery with filters, QR connections, Inspiration Sessions, Messages and a client card with visit history.

### Is Nailfolio free?

Stylists can start with Free Demo without a card: up to 5 photos, up to 2 connected clients and 3 pinned styles. Free Demo does not expire. Pro includes an unlimited gallery and client list, plus 90 days of visit history; compare the other paid plans on the pricing page. The 14-day trial is available once per account and requires a card at activation. After 14 days, the subscription starts unless you cancel first. A client account is free.

### How do I get started with Nailfolio?

A stylist or client opens nailfolio.app in a phone browser on Android or iPhone and signs in with Google or an email link.

### What devices does Nailfolio work on?

Stylists and clients can use all Nailfolio features in a phone browser on Android and iPhone. There is no separate desktop app. The Android app is in preparation.

### Is my data safe?

Connections to the app are encrypted. Stripe handles payments. See the Privacy Policy for information about service providers and data processing.

### How can I contact Nailfolio?

A stylist or client can use the contact form, email https://nailfolio.app/contact or message @nailfolio on Instagram.

### How do I sign in if there's no password?

A stylist or client signs in with Google or an email link. To receive a link, enter an email address and open the link in the message. No password is needed.

### The magic sign-in link isn't arriving, what should I do?

A stylist or client should check Spam and Promotions. A sign-in link is valid for one hour; after that, request a new one or sign in with Google. If the problem continues, use the contact form and include the email address used to sign in.

### Can I sign in on several devices at once?

A stylist or client can use the same account across all their devices at once. Sign in the same way on each one: with a login link sent to the same email address or with Google. Active sessions can be checked and signed out in the Signed-in devices section of Account.

### How do I switch between the nail artist and client roles?

One person can use Nailfolio as both a stylist and a client. In Account, select Settings, then Switch role. Each role keeps its own data, galleries and settings.

### The app isn't working or loads slowly, what should I do?

A stylist or client should close and reopen Nailfolio, then check the internet connection. If the problem continues, send a report with the phone model, operating system and error description.

### How do I report an inappropriate photo?

A stylist or client opens the photo menu (three dots), selects “Report” and chooses a reason. The report goes to the moderation team. If a moderator takes action on the photo and the author's email address is available, the author receives the decision by email. The person who submitted the report does not receive an outcome notification.

## For nail artists

### How do I add nail photos to my Nailfolio gallery?

Open the app, go to the Add tab, take a photo or choose from your gallery, tag colors and features, then publish.

### What are pinned photos?

Pinned photos are your best work, displayed at the top of your public profile. The number of pins depends on your plan.

### How do I generate a QR code for clients?

You have one permanent QR code on the Connection screen. Show it to your client on your phone or print it and display it at your station. Your client scans it, opens your profile and sends a connection request for you to accept.

### How can a client send me inspiration before the visit?

Your client creates an Inspiration Session, chooses photos from your gallery, adds one note and shares the collection with you in one tap. You will find it in the Session Inbox.

### How do I change my pricing plan?

Open Your account and plan, then Plan. Before changing a paid plan, you will see the amount and billing consequences. The change takes effect after the displayed difference is settled. If you switch to Free Demo, your current plan remains active until the end of the paid period.

### Can I add nail artist accounts?

Choose Studio and invite stylists by email. After accepting and signing in normally, they use the active salon plan’s capabilities in their own accounts. Their work joins the shared gallery with author credits; each can turn this off in her profile. A client connected with one member can browse the whole gallery and contact any contributing stylist. You can display your permanent QR code at reception as the salon’s code: once you accept the request, the client can see the work of all team members who share photos with the salon and message each of them.

### What happens to my photos when I downgrade?

Your photos and data stay in your account, and connected clients retain access to the full gallery. After a paid plan ends, the public profile shows up to 3 pinned styles, and Free Demo blocks adding photos beyond 5 or connecting more than 2 clients. Nothing is deleted.

### How does subscription auto-renewal work?

After the 14-day trial, your subscription renews automatically. Manage your plan in account settings.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Your account and plan, then Plan and subscription management. You can cancel renewal in the Stripe portal. Your current plan remains active until the end of the paid period, then Free Demo limits apply. Your account, data and photos remain, and connected clients keep access to the gallery.

### My payment didn't go through, what should I do?

Check that your card is active and has sufficient funds. Go to Account → Subscription → Manage subscription and update your card details. Stripe will automatically retry the payment. If the problem persists, use the contact form.

### I'd like a refund, how does that work?

During the 14-day Pro trial, you can cancel your subscription at no charge. If you have already been charged, submit a request within 14 days. After it has been reviewed, you will receive a response about the refund.

### A staff member has left, how do I revoke their access?

The owner can remove a stylist, and a member can leave. The stylist returns to their own plan’s capabilities. Photos and client data stay on their account, but adding beyond their own plan’s limits is blocked.

### How do I reply to a client in the app?

Open the Messages tab, there you'll see conversations with clients you're connected with.

### Do I have to add a phone number to my profile?

No, the field is optional. If you fill it in, clients will see it on your public profile.

## For clients

### How do I find a nail artist?

Scan your stylist’s QR code at the salon and sign in with Google or an email link to request a connection. Your stylist controls access: once she accepts your request, you can see her full gallery. Filter her work by color and features to find what you have in mind.

### Can I like nail artists?

Yes. Select “Like stylist” on the stylist’s profile. You can find her later in your collection.

### How do I like a design?

Select the heart on a photo in your stylist’s gallery. You’ll find liked designs in “Your collection”.

### What is connecting with a nail artist?

Connecting with a stylist lets you see her full gallery, send Inspiration Sessions and write Messages. In an active Studio salon, one connection also opens the work of all contributing stylists. You can send inspiration or a message to any of them without another connection request.

### Do I have to pay for an account?

No. Your client account is free. You can browse galleries and like any design or stylist at no cost.

### How do I delete my account?

Open Account, select Settings, then Delete account. Your account will be deactivated and your data stored securely. You have 90 days to change your mind: sign in again and restore the account with one click. You can request permanent data deletion at any time under Article 17 GDPR by writing to https://nailfolio.app/contact.

### Does the artist’s code expire or work only once?

No. Your stylist’s QR code is permanent: the same code is displayed at her station and on her screen. Scan it with your regular camera to open your stylist’s profile and send a connection request. Your stylist can accept it with one tap.

### I see a message that I'm already connected with this nail artist. What does that mean?

It means you already have an active connection with your stylist, so you do not need to scan her code again. Open Dashboard, then Connected stylists, to see her full gallery. You can connect with up to 3 stylists at a time.

### My photo has disappeared from the gallery, why?

A photo may be hidden if it violates the content rules, for example if it contains personal data, faces or content unrelated to nails. You'll receive the reason for the decision by email. To appeal, reply to that message.

### Can I message my stylist?

Yes. Use Messages once you are connected with your stylist.

## Technical

### What photo formats are supported?

Nailfolio supports JPG, PNG and WEBP formats. Maximum file size is 10 MB. Photos are automatically optimised.

### How does color tagging work?

Photos can be assigned one or more colors from the available palette.

### Is data encrypted?

Connections to the app are encrypted.

### What are style features?

Features are tags describing techniques, effects and styles, e.g. French, Ombre, Chrome, Floral. The app has a set of predefined features in four categories.

### How do gallery filters work?

Photos in a stylist’s gallery can be filtered by color, style and nail length. Filters work together, and results update immediately.

### Does Nailfolio have an API?

Nailfolio does not offer a public API. Submit integration questions using the contact form.

### Does Nailfolio work offline?

The gallery and Inspiration Sessions require an internet connection. Nailfolio has no offline mode.
