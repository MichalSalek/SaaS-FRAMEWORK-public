## Nailfolio on your phone: nail portfolio and client history

Access your work gallery, Inspiration Sessions and visit history in your phone’s browser. The Android app will be available on Google Play.

## Gallery, Inspiration Sessions and client history

The stylist maintains a work gallery and visit history. A connected client chooses photos for an Inspiration Session and shares it before the visit.

### Adding nail design photos

Add a nail design photo and select its color, style and nail length. Once published, the photo is available in your gallery.

For nail artists

### Connecting with a stylist via QR code

The client scans the QR code, signs in with Google or an email link and sends a connection request. The stylist confirms the request to give access to the full gallery.

For nail artists and clients

### Color, style, and length filters

Select a nail color, style and length. The gallery displays photos matching the selected criteria.

For clients

### Inspiration Sessions before a visit

The client chooses gallery photos, adds notes and shares an Inspiration Session with the stylist. The stylist sees it in the Session Clipboard. A shared session stays active for 7 days.

For nail artists and clients

### Works on your phone and tablet

Use your phone or tablet. Your gallery and data sync automatically.

For nail artists

### Messages with connected clients

In Messages, you communicate with connected clients. You can also open a conversation from the client card.

For stylists and clients

## How Nailfolio works in the nail salon

Create an account, add photos or connect with a stylist, then prepare an Inspiration Session.

### Create an account

Open nailfolio.app in your phone’s browser. Sign in with Google or a link sent by email.

### Pick your role

On a stylist account, add photos and select colors and features. On a client account, scan the QR code and send a connection request to browse the full gallery once confirmed.

### Share an Inspiration Session

The client chooses gallery photos and shares an Inspiration Session. The stylist can review the photos and notes before the visit.

- No ads in the app
- Access in your phone’s browser
- No cross-app tracking
- Database stored in the EU

### When will the app be on Google Play, and how do I start today?

Nailfolio is available in your phone’s browser on Android and iPhone. The Android app is being prepared for publication on Google Play. A native iOS app is not currently available.

### Will the app be free?

Clients always use it for free, with no card and no other fees. Stylists start on the Free plan, also with no card and no time limit. When they need more, they can choose Pro, Pro Marka or Studio. I price the Scale plan individually. Pro, Pro Marka and Studio come with a 30-day trial that requires a card. The subscription starts once the trial ends.

### Does the app work without internet?

Features such as the gallery and Inspiration Sessions require an internet connection. Nailfolio has no offline mode.

### Does the app collect data for advertising?

Nailfolio does not use marketing cookies or advertising pixels and does not share data with advertisers. You can turn off analytics in the cookie banner. The app database is stored in the EU. Information about service providers and data processing is in the privacy policy.

### Which devices are supported?

Nailfolio works straight away in the browser on any phone. The Android app (version 10 or newer) is coming to Google Play soon.

### Does the client have to install the app?

Clients can use Nailfolio in their phone’s browser. An account is needed for likes and Inspiration Sessions, and a confirmed connection with the stylist gives access to the full gallery. Sign-in uses Google or an email link. The stylist’s public profile is available without an account.

### Can I use Nailfolio as a client, without a stylist account?

Yes. Nailfolio lets you have two accounts at once: you can be a stylist running your portfolio and a client of another stylist. The accounts are separate, each with its own history and inspiration sessions.

### What happens when I change my phone?

You sign in by email on the new device, and all your data (gallery, clients, inspiration sessions) is right there in the cloud. You can stay signed in on several devices at once, for example your phone and a tablet in the salon.
