## Nailfolio on your phone: nail portfolio and client history

Nailfolio helps nail professionals and clients agree on a design before the appointment. Photos, a note and Messages help save time at the salon. Use it in your phone’s browser.

## Gallery, Inspiration Sessions and client history

Add your work and keep client cards. Your client chooses photos before the visit.

### Add and organize your photos

Add photos of your work and tag them by color, style and nail length. Published photos appear in your gallery. Pin selected styles to your public profile, which anyone with the link can view.

### Show your QR code to your client

Show your client your permanent QR code. When she sends a connection request, accept it to give her access to your full gallery.

### Filter photos by style

Once connected, your client filters photos in your gallery by color, style and nail length.

### Share an Inspiration Session before the visit

Your client chooses photos and shares an Inspiration Session with you. She can add a note. You will find the session under “Sessions from clients”; it stays active for 30 days.

### Scans your QR code and signs in

Your client scans your QR code and sends a connection request. Once you accept, they can see your full gallery.

### Message clients and keep their cards

In Messages, you can talk to clients even before connecting. Paid plans let you keep private cards for connected clients, add notes and view visit history.

## How Nailfolio works in the nail salon

Create an account, add photos of your work and show your client the QR code. Your client chooses a design before the visit.

### Create an account

Click “Try Nailfolio” to go to registration.

### Add photos of your work

Add photos of your work and tag them by color, style and nail length.

### Show your client the QR code

Your client scans the QR code, connects with you and chooses photos from your gallery before the visit.

- No ads in the app
- Your work and clients in one place
- You choose which work appears on your public profile.
- Client cards are private to each stylist.

### When will the app be on Google Play, and how do I start today?

Nailfolio is available in your phone’s browser on Android and iPhone. The Android app is being prepared for publication on Google Play. A native iOS app is not currently available.

### How much does Nailfolio cost?

A client account is free. You can try Free Demo without a card; see the pricing page for paid-plan prices and details.

### Does the app work without internet?

Features such as the gallery and Inspiration Sessions require an internet connection. Nailfolio has no offline mode.

### Does the app collect data for advertising?

Nailfolio does not use marketing cookies or advertising pixels and does not share data with advertisers. You can turn off analytics in the cookie banner. See the Privacy Policy for information about service providers and data processing.

### Which devices are supported?

Nailfolio runs in your phone's browser; you do not need to install an app.

### Does the client have to install the app?

Clients can use Nailfolio in their phone’s browser. An account is needed for likes and Inspiration Sessions, and a confirmed connection with the stylist gives access to the full gallery. Sign-in uses Google or an email link. The stylist’s public profile is available without an account.

### Can I use Nailfolio as a client, without a stylist account?

Yes. You can use a free client account without creating a stylist account. You can also have both.

### What happens when I change my phone?

On a new device, sign in with the same email address. You can use Nailfolio on several devices, such as a phone and a tablet.
