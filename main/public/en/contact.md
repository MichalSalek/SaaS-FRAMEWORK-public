## Contact us

Use the form or send an email about your account, app features, pricing or partnerships.

### When will I receive a reply?

You will receive a reply within one business day.

### What should I include in a bug report?

Describe what is not working and when the error occurs. Include your phone model and browser. If possible, attach a screenshot to your email.

### What language can I write in?

Contact is available in Polish, English and German. The reply will use the language of your message.

### I have a pricing question. Where should I write?

Select “Plans and pricing” or email kontakt@nailfolio.app. You can also ask about Studio and Scale plans there.

### How can I request an offer for a salon network?

Select “Partnership or collaboration” or email kontakt@nailfolio.app to ask about a custom Scale offer.

### Where is Nailfolio from?

Nailfolio is an independently developed app from Kraków.
