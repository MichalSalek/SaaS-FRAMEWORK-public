## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my plan at any time?

Changes between paid plans take effect once the prorated difference is paid. If you switch to Free, your current paid plan remains active until the end of the paid period.

### How does the 14-day trial work?

If your account is eligible for the one-time trial, it lasts 14 days and requires a card. After the trial, your selected plan becomes a paid subscription unless you cancel beforehand. Cancel during the trial to avoid a charge. Free Demo does not expire.

### Which payment methods are available?

Stripe handles payments. You'll see the available methods in the payment window.

### Do clients pay anything?

No. Your client scans the QR code and sends a connection request. After you approve it, she browses the full gallery and shares inspiration. You control access, and clients use the gallery and Inspiration Sessions free of charge.

### What does Free Demo include, and what does Pro include?

With Free Demo, you can try the gallery and client connections. Pro gives you an unlimited gallery and client connections, plus visit history. See the plan comparison above for other differences.

### Are my photos and client data secure?

The app connection is encrypted, and Stripe handles payments. See the Privacy Policy for information about external services and data processing.

### What happens to my photos after I cancel?

Photos remain in your account. When the paid plan ends, Free Demo limits apply: the public profile shows up to 3 pinned styles, and adding photos beyond 5 is blocked. Connected clients retain gallery access.

### Will I receive an invoice?

Yes. You can enter your business details at checkout. You can find invoices in the billing section of your account.

### What does a stylist get by joining a Studio team?

With an active Studio or Scale plan, each stylist uses the plan's features on their own account. The salon profile shows pinned work from stylists who have chosen to participate, with each author credited. A client connected to one of them can see the full salon gallery and send an Inspiration Session or message to any participating stylist. Client cards and private notes are not shared.

### How is the payment currency selected?

The currency is selected automatically for your country, independently of the app language. Poland uses PLN, euro area countries use EUR, and other countries, including Canada, use USD. You enter invoice details in the payment form. An existing subscription keeps its currency when you travel or change the language.
