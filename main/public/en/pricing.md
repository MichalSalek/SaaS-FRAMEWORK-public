## Nailfolio pricing and plan comparison

Nailfolio connects your portfolio with how you prepare for every visit: your client's gallery picks, the conversation and her recent visit history, all in one place.

- 30 days of Pro free
- Fixed fee, 0% commission on clients
- Free plan forever, no card

| Plan | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Free | demo: 5 photos, 2 clients, no card |
| Pro | solo stylist, unlimited gallery |
| Pro Marka | Pro + lifelong client history and brand color |
| Studio | salon, whole team included, up to 10 accounts, no per-seat fees |
| Scale | salon chains, SLA, custom pricing |

Current prices (PLN, billed monthly or yearly with a 20% discount): https://nailfolio.app/en/pricing. Every paid plan starts with a 30-day trial (card required at activation, Stripe). The Free plan needs no card. Clients always use Nailfolio for free.

## Features available during the Pro trial

- Unlimited gallery photos instead of 5 on the Free plan
- Unlimited connected clients instead of 2 on the Free plan
- Inspiration session: the client picks photos with notes, and you have the full set before the visit.
- Your own profile link, e.g. nailfolio.app/u/anna-nails, to show every client.

Use Pro features for 30 days. The trial requires a card. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid a charge.

A paid subscription starts after 30 days unless canceled beforehand. Free has no charge or time limit and requires no card.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my plan at any time?

Yes. A higher plan takes effect immediately: e.g. switching from Pro to Pro Marka mid-month, we charge only the prorated difference. A lower plan takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

### What happens after the 30 free days of Pro?

After 30 days the subscription starts automatically at your chosen plan. You get a reminder email 7 days before the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial you pay nothing, and your photos and data stay. You can stay on the Free plan for as long as you like.

### Which payment methods are available?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard) and BLIK via Stripe. Card details never touch Nailfolio servers.

### Do clients pay anything?

No. The client scans the QR code and sends a connection request. Once the stylist accepts it, the client sees the full gallery and shares inspiration sessions. The stylist controls access, and the client uses the gallery and inspiration sessions at no cost.

### How do the 30 free days of Pro work?

A new stylist account starts on the Free plan, no card. Every paid plan comes with a 30-day free trial: you add a card at the start, and the subscription begins only after the trial. Your photos and data stay either way.

### Are my photos and client data secure?

The app connection is encrypted, the Nailfolio database is hosted in the European Union and Stripe handles payments. See the privacy policy for information about external services.

### What happens to my photos after I cancel?

Photos remain in your account. When the paid plan ends, Free limits apply: the public profile shows up to 3 pinned designs, and adding photos beyond 5 is blocked. Connected clients retain gallery access.

### Do I get a VAT invoice?

Yes. At checkout you turn on "Company VAT invoice" and enter your company name, address and tax ID. From then on a VAT invoice with your details appears on every payment, in your billing panel. It covers Polish sole proprietorship, civil partnership and limited company.
