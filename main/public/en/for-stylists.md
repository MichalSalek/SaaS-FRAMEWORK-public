## Design selection and client notes before a visit

Before your client takes a seat, she browses your portfolio and chooses a design for the visit.

**Problem:** Your client shows you a design from the web that needs to fit the length of her nails.
**Solution:** Show your client your own work and narrow the choices with length, color and style filters.

**Problem:** On Instagram, finding one specific design in a flood of posts is hard.
**Solution:** Tags help your client narrow the gallery by color, length and style instead of scrolling through post after post.

**Problem:** Before the visit, photos from the web and earlier decisions are hard to find.
**Solution:** Your client shares selected photos in an Inspiration Session and can add a note. You can review the session before the appointment.

## Questions about Nailfolio in your work

### How does Nailfolio differ from Instagram?

In Nailfolio, your client browses your work with color, style and length filters. She can select photos, add a note and share an Inspiration Session with you. Paid plans also include visit history for your clients.

### How does a client access the gallery?

Your client scans your QR code, signs in with Google or an email link and sends a connection request. Once you accept it, she can access your full gallery. Your public profile with selected work is available without signing in.

### How do Free Demo and Pro differ?

Free Demo lets you add up to 5 photos and connect with up to 2 clients. Pro has no limit on photos or client connections. You also get access to visit history.

### How does the paid-plan trial work?

If you have not used a trial before, your first paid plan starts a 14-day trial. You need to add a card to start it. To prevent a paid subscription from starting after the trial, cancel it in the Stripe portal before it ends.

### I already use Booksy/Fresha. Is Nailfolio an alternative?

Nailfolio is for presenting your work and preparing for visits. Booksy and Fresha offer appointment booking, which Nailfolio does not provide. You can use these tools alongside each other. Compare Booksy, Fresha and Instagram.

### What happens to my photos if I cancel my subscription?

Photos remain in your account. When the paid plan ends, Free Demo limits apply: up to 3 pinned styles on the public profile, 5 photos and 2 connected clients. Above a limit, you cannot add new photos or connections. Connected clients retain gallery access.

### Does a client have to install an app to see my entire gallery?

The public profile with selected work is available without an account. The full gallery, QR connection and Inspiration Sessions require a free client account. Nailfolio works in a phone browser, with no installation needed.

### I'm switching from another app. Will you help me import my gallery?

Contact support before moving your gallery to discuss importing it. Available assistance depends on the format of your photos and data from the previous app.

### Will my client see that I pay for an app?

No. Your clients cannot access your subscription, invoices or payment settings.

### Can I message my clients in the app?

Yes. In Messages, you can talk to a client without connecting first or sharing your private phone number. Adding a phone number to your public profile is optional.

### I don't have a fixed station, I work at my clients' homes, is this for me?

Yes. You can show your QR code wherever you work. Once the connection is confirmed, your client can access your gallery.
