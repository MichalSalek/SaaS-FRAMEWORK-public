## Design selection and client notes before a visit

Before the client sits in the chair, she looks at your portfolio and picks a design for the visit.

**Problem:** A client shows a design from the internet that needs to be adapted to her nail length.
**Solution:** Show the client your work and narrow the choice with length, color and style filters.

**Problem:** On Instagram, finding one specific design in a flood of posts is hard.
**Solution:** In Nailfolio every photo has a color, length and style. Your client narrows the choice instead of scrolling through post after post.

**Problem:** Before the visit, photos from the web and earlier decisions are hard to find.
**Solution:** The client shares selected photos and notes in an Inspiration Session. You can review them before the visit.

## Stylists' most common questions

### How does Nailfolio differ from Instagram?

In Nailfolio, clients browse your work using color, style and length filters. They can select photos, add notes and share an Inspiration Session. Paid plans also include client history.

### How does a client access the gallery?

The client scans your QR code, signs in with Google or an email link and sends a connection request. Once you accept it, they can access the full gallery. The public profile with selected work is available without signing in.

### How do Free and Pro differ?

Free has no charge or time limit. Pro includes unlimited gallery photos and connected clients, plus visit history from the last 90 days. Inspiration Sessions are available on every plan. See pricing for prices and other limits.

### How does the Pro trial work?

The Pro trial lasts 30 days and requires a card. Cancel in the Stripe portal before it ends to avoid a charge. Otherwise, a paid subscription starts after the trial.

### I already use Booksy/Fresha. Is Nailfolio an alternative?

Nailfolio is for presenting your work and preparing for visits. Booksy and Fresha offer appointment booking, which Nailfolio does not provide. You can use these tools alongside each other. Compare Booksy, Fresha and Instagram.

### What happens to my photos if I cancel my subscription?

Photos remain in your account. When the paid plan ends, Free limits apply: up to 3 public profile pins, 5 photos and 2 connected clients. Above a limit, you cannot add new photos or connections. Connected clients retain gallery access.

### Does a client have to install an app to see my entire gallery?

The public profile with selected work is available without an account. The full gallery, QR connection and Inspiration Sessions require a free client account. Nailfolio works in a phone browser, with no installation needed.

### I'm switching from another app. Will you help me import my gallery?

Contact support before moving your gallery to discuss importing it. Available assistance depends on the format of your photos and data from the previous app.

### Will my client see that I pay for an app?

No. Clients cannot access your subscription, invoices or payment settings.

### Can I message my clients in the app?

Yes. In the Messages tab you reply to a client you're connected with, without sharing a private number or Messenger. The phone number on your public profile is optional, you add it only if you want to.

### I don't have a fixed station, I work at my clients' homes, is this for me?

Yes. You can show the QR code on your phone wherever you work. After the connection is confirmed, the client can access your gallery.
