## Questions people ask most

### What is Nailfolio?

Nailfolio is an app for nail stylists and their clients. It includes a work gallery with filters, QR connections, Inspiration Sessions and Messages. Paid plans also give stylists access to client visit history. The app works in a phone browser.

### How does Nailfolio differ from an Instagram gallery?

In Nailfolio, clients browse your work using color, style and length filters. Once connected, they can select photos and share an Inspiration Session with notes. Paid plans also include visit history.

### What does Free include?

Yes. You sign up without a card and start on the Free plan: 5 gallery photos, a QR code and basic filters, free forever. Every paid plan comes with a 30-day free trial: you add a card when the trial starts, and the subscription begins only after it ends (cancel anytime). Zero commission on your clients.

### What does the client get after scanning the QR code?

After signing in, the client sends a connection request. You confirm it with one tap, so you control access to your full gallery with filters for color, style and length. Once confirmed, the client browses your work, can like designs and send you an inspiration session before the visit.

### What about my photos: who owns them?

You retain the rights to your photos. Nailfolio does not sell them or use them in ads. The Nailfolio database is hosted in the European Union. You can deactivate your account and restore it within 90 days.

### How do I set up a portfolio?

Create an account, add photos and tag color, style and length. Then share your QR code with the client and approve their connection request.
