## Questions people ask most

### What is Nailfolio?

Nailfolio helps you manage your work gallery, connect with clients by QR code, receive their Inspiration Sessions and talk in Messages. Paid plans also include client visit history. The app works in a phone browser.

### How does Nailfolio differ from an Instagram gallery?

In Nailfolio, your client browses your work with color, style and length filters. Once connected, she can select photos and share an Inspiration Session with a note. Paid plans also include visit history.

### What does Free Demo include?

With Free Demo, you can add up to 5 photos and connect with up to 2 clients. See the pricing page for paid-plan details.

### What does the client get after scanning the QR code?

After signing in, your client sends a connection request. You approve it with one tap, so you control access to your full gallery with color, style and length filters. Once connected, she can browse your work, like designs and send you an Inspiration Session before the visit.

### What about my photos: who owns them?

You retain the rights to your photos. Nailfolio does not sell them or use them in ads. After closing your account, you have 90 days to restore it along with your photos.

### How do I set up a portfolio?

Create an account, add photos and tag color, style and length. Then show your QR code to your client and approve her connection request.
