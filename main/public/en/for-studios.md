## Owner account and team membership

The owner manages the salon account. The team list confirms membership and shows stylists’ profiles after invitation acceptance. It does not share the salon gallery or data with them.

## Salon gallery, client history and team list

Studio includes full client history and a team list. Inviting a stylist confirms their membership; it does not share access to the salon account.

### Gallery on the salon account

The owner adds work photos to the salon account’s gallery. Team membership does not give a stylist access to that gallery.

### Salon team list

After accepting an invitation, the stylist appears on the team list with their name and profile photo.

### Inspiration Sessions on the salon account

A connected client can share an Inspiration Session with the salon account. Team membership does not give a stylist access to those sessions.

### Owner's panel: the whole salon in one view

Use the owner dashboard to send invitations, check their status and remove people from the team list.

## Three steps to launch your nail salon on Nailfolio

Create a Studio account, add photos to the gallery and invite stylists to the team list. Then connect the account with clients using the QR code.

### Create a Studio account and invite your nail artists

Choose Studio. Invite stylists who already have a Nailfolio account. After accepting, their name and photo appear on the team list.

### Add photos to the salon gallery

As the owner, add photos, tag them and select work for the public profile. Invited stylists do not gain access to the salon account’s gallery.

### Clients scan the QR at the salon

Show the client the salon account’s QR code. After signing in, they send a connection request. Once you approve it, they can browse the gallery and share Inspiration Sessions.

## Questions about Studio plan

### How many stylists can I add?

Studio includes the owner and up to 9 people on the team list, with no per-seat fees. A stylist must have their own account and accept the invitation. Membership does not provide access to the salon gallery or data.

### Can clients see who did which design?

The team list shows the stylist’s name and photo after invitation acceptance. The salon gallery is managed from the owner’s account.

### Can invited stylists access the salon gallery?

No. Accepting an invitation confirms membership and allows the stylist’s name and photo to appear on the team list. It does not grant access to the salon account or gallery.

### How much does the Studio plan cost?

See pricing for the current price. Studio includes up to 10 team accounts, the owner dashboard and a shared salon profile. For a larger team, contact support about Scale.

### Can I cancel the Studio plan?

Yes, at any time from your account. After cancellation your photos, clients and session history remain: you move to the Free plan.

### What happens when a nail artist leaves the team?

The owner can remove a stylist from the team list. This does not delete their separate Nailfolio account. Data stored on the salon account remains there.

### Can I test Studio before purchasing?

Studio has a 30-day trial with a card required at activation. You can test salon account features and team invitations. Joining the team does not give a stylist access to salon data.

### What about a large team or several locations?

Studio covers one location and up to 10 accounts with no per-seat fees. Contact support to discuss Scale and an individual offer for larger teams or multiple locations.

### Can stylists see messages on the salon account?

No. Team membership does not share Messages from the salon account. The owner sees conversations on their own account, not private conversations from separate stylist accounts.
