## Your whole team’s work in your salon gallery

Stylists keep their own accounts and photo authorship. You choose the salon showcase, and clients have one place to find inspiration before their visit.

### Shared salon gallery

Each stylist adds photos to her own account. The salon gallery shows the team’s work with each author’s name; clients can choose a stylist and filter by colour, style and length.

### Salon profile with your own address

Choose a readable salon profile address and a showcase from the whole team’s gallery. Without an account, clients see your showcase and each stylist’s pinned work, always with its author.

### One connection to the whole salon

Once any stylist approves the QR connection, the client can browse the full salon gallery. She can send an Inspiration Session or message to any stylist contributing to it.

### Own work and private conversations

Photos belong to the stylist who uploaded them. Each stylist keeps her own clients, notes and conversations; neither you nor her colleagues can access them.

## From a photo at the station to inspiration before the visit

## Questions about Studio plan

### How many stylists can I add?

With the Studio plan, you can invite up to 9 stylists, in addition to the owner's account. Each person accepts an invitation and uses their own account. While Studio or Scale is active, team members can use its features without an additional subscription.

### Can clients see who did which design?

Yes. Every work in the salon gallery shows the author’s name, avatar and profile link. Clients can also filter to one stylist’s work.

### Can invited stylists access the salon gallery?

Yes, members can browse the shared salon gallery. Each edits and deletes only her own photos. Only you choose the showcase at the top of the salon profile.

### How much does the Studio plan cost?

See the pricing page for the current price. Studio includes up to 10 accounts including yours, a salon dashboard and a shared salon profile. Contact us about Scale for a larger team.

### Can I cancel the Studio plan?

Yes, you can cancel on the Plan page. When the paid plan ends, the salon gallery and profile become unavailable and stylists return to their own plans. Photos and direct client connections stay in their accounts; salon-derived connections end.

### What happens when a nail artist leaves the team?

Her work leaves the salon gallery and showcase, but remains in her account with clients directly connected to her. Salon-derived connections end. She returns to her own plan without deleting photos. Clients keep salon access if they also have a direct connection with another team member.

### Can I test Studio before purchasing?

You can try Studio under the trial terms shown in pricing. Team members receive plan capabilities on their own accounts when the salon subscription is an active paid subscription.

### What about a large team or several locations?

Studio covers one location and up to 10 accounts with no per-seat fees. Contact support to discuss Scale and an individual offer for larger teams or multiple locations.

### Can I read my stylists’ client conversations?

No. Each stylist has her own conversations and client cards. The shared gallery gives neither you nor colleagues access to that information.

### What can a client see before and after connecting?

Without an account, she sees your salon showcase and all contributing stylists’ pinned work. After an approved QR connection with any of them or you, she can access the full salon gallery and send an Inspiration Session or message to any contributing stylist.

### Who owns the photos in the salon gallery?

They belong to the stylist who uploaded them. Joining enables contributions to the salon gallery; she can switch them off in her profile. Neither you nor colleagues can edit or delete her photos. When she leaves, her work stays in her own account.
