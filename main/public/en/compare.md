## How is Nailfolio different from Booksy, Fresha and Instagram?

Compare booking apps, social media and Nailfolio by purpose. In Nailfolio, you maintain a work gallery and visit history and receive Inspiration Sessions with notes from clients.

## Comparison of features and purpose

Bookings, photo publishing and visit preparation serve different purposes.

| | Nailfolio | Booksy / Fresha | Instagram / Pinterest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it's for | Visit prep and client history | Bookings and the appointment calendar | Reach and online presence |
| What your client sees | Your gallery with color, style and length filters | Time slots and services with a price list | Posts, reels and photos |
| Client history | Notes, messages, inspiration sessions and visit history | Appointment history | No dedicated client card |
| Cost for you | Free plan or paid subscription, no commission from clients | A subscription, and with some tools a commission on every new client acquired through their platform | Free account, optional paid advertising |

You can use a booking app alongside Nailfolio. Add a booking link to your public profile. In Nailfolio, clients choose photos for Inspiration Sessions and stylists maintain visit history.

## Nailfolio and appointment booking apps

- Booksy is used for bookings and appointment calendars. Nailfolio is used to present work, choose inspiration and maintain client history. Bookings take place outside Nailfolio.
- A client can book through Booksy, then choose photos from your Nailfolio gallery. After connecting, they can share an Inspiration Session and discuss details in Messages.

Nailfolio does not broker bookings or charge commission from clients.

## Nailfolio and sharing work on Instagram

- On Instagram, you publish photos, reels and stories and communicate with followers.
- In Nailfolio, clients filter photos of your work by nail color, style and length. They choose photos for an Inspiration Session, which they can share before a visit.

You can add your Nailfolio public profile link to Instagram.

## Pinterest photos and a gallery of your own work

- On Pinterest, clients browse inspiration from many creators. Before creating a design, discuss how it fits the nail length and shape and the available materials.
- In Nailfolio, clients choose from your previous work. They can share selected photos and notes in an Inspiration Session to discuss the details before a visit.

## Gallery and preparation for a visit

In Nailfolio, you review photos chosen by the client, communicate in Messages and record visit history in the client card.

## The most common questions about Nailfolio next to other tools

### Does Nailfolio replace Booksy?

No. Bookings take place outside Nailfolio. In Nailfolio, you maintain a gallery, receive Inspiration Sessions and record client history. You can add a booking link to your public profile.

### Do I have to give up Instagram?

No. You can continue publishing work on Instagram and add a link to Nailfolio. In Nailfolio, clients browse a filtered gallery and choose photos for Inspiration Sessions.

### How is Nailfolio different from Pinterest?

Pinterest includes photos by many creators. In Nailfolio, clients browse their stylist’s work, filter it and choose photos for Inspiration Sessions.

### How much does it cost compared to Booksy or Fresha?

You start for free: 5 photos, 2 connected clients and no card. Pro is a flat monthly fee with no commission, you'll find the current price on the pricing page. Booking apps usually require a subscription, and some also take a commission on every new client acquired through their platform.

### What can I use Nailfolio for alongside other apps?

In Nailfolio, you can keep your work gallery, photos chosen by clients before a visit, messages and visit history. Clients share Inspiration Sessions after connecting with a stylist.

### Can I import photos from Instagram or Booksy?

Use the contact form before moving photos. You can ask for help preparing the gallery and assigning colors and features to photos.
